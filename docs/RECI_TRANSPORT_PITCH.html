Platform Capabilities

Features & Technical Implementation

Page 2 of 4
01

Conversational Vehicle Search

Customers describe their trip in plain language — "a family SUV for a week from the 10th" — and the AI parses intent, dates, and category automatically. No form-filling required.

02

5-Step Guided Booking Flow

Vehicle selection → Extras (child seat, GPS, insurance) → Driver details → Stripe payment → Confirmation with booking reference. Works on all devices without an account.

03

Stripe Payment Integration

PCI-compliant card payments via Stripe PaymentElement. Supports all major cards and local European methods. Webhook-driven confirmation ensures no booking is missed.

04

RECI Rewards Loyalty Programme

Customers earn points on every booking, redeemable for rental discounts. Tiers (Bronze → Silver → Gold) drive repeat bookings and customer retention automatically.

05

AI Damage Detection (Vision)

Staff upload vehicle photos at pickup and return. Claude Vision analyses the images and generates a structured damage report — cutting dispute resolution time significantly.

06

Full Admin Portal

Separate, secure back-office portal for staff and admins. Manage fleet, bookings, pricing, customers, maintenance schedules, and user accounts — all in one place.

Admin Portal Guide

Back-Office Portal — Full Walkthrough

Page 4 of 4

The Admin Portal is a fully separate, secure back-office application. It runs at its own URL, requires staff or admin credentials, and is invisible to customers. Everything your operations team needs is in one place — no spreadsheets, no phone calls, no guesswork.

Core Modules
01

Dashboard

Live snapshot of the business at a glance.

  • Active bookings count & revenue today
  • Fleet utilisation rate
  • Pending returns and pickups
  • Recent booking activity feed
02

Bookings

Full booking lifecycle management.

  • View all bookings — filter by status, date, vehicle
  • Confirm, cancel, or modify bookings
  • See payment status (Stripe) per booking
  • Access driver details and extras selected
03

Fleet Management

Every vehicle, every detail, in one view.

  • Add, edit, or deactivate vehicles
  • Set vehicle category, features, images
  • Block availability (maintenance, private use)
  • View live availability calendar per vehicle
04

Customers

Full customer account visibility.

  • Browse all registered customer accounts
  • View booking history per customer
  • Check loyalty tier & points balance
  • Manage corporate account assignments
05

Pricing & Extras

Full control over rates and add-ons.

  • Set base rate per vehicle category
  • Configure corporate pricing overrides
  • Manage extras (child seat, GPS, insurance…)
  • Set seasonal pricing rules per date range
06

Maintenance (AI)

AI-assisted vehicle servicing.

  • Log service events and mileage
  • Upload vehicle photos for AI damage scan
  • Claude Vision classifies damage severity
  • AI generates structured service notes
